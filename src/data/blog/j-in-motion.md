**Web Design | Full-Stack Development | Requirements Gathering | Stakeholder Collaboration | UI/UX Design**

![Animated walkthrough of the J in Motion website](/jinmotionsite.gif)

Designed and developed a website for J in Motion, translating the organization's goals and vision into a professional, user-friendly digital experience. Managed the development process from initial requirements gathering and design conceptualization through implementation, stakeholder feedback, and iterative refinement.

Worked closely with stakeholders to understand their needs, establish project requirements, and identify priorities for the website's functionality, content, and visual identity. Translated these requirements into an intuitive, responsive design that balanced usability, aesthetics, and business objectives.

Throughout development, maintained an iterative feedback loop with stakeholders, presenting design updates, gathering input, and incorporating revisions to ensure the final product aligned with their expectations.

## Key Contributions

- **Requirements Gathering:** Collaborated directly with stakeholders to identify business objectives, target audiences, functional requirements, and design preferences.
- **UI/UX Design:** Designed a cohesive visual interface with an emphasis on intuitive navigation, accessibility, responsive layouts, and consistent branding.
- **Website Development:** Translated design concepts and stakeholder requirements into a functional website, implementing page layouts, navigation, and interactive elements.
- **Iterative Development:** Established ongoing feedback loops, using stakeholder reviews to refine designs, prioritize changes, and improve the user experience.
- **Project Management:** Coordinated development milestones, managed evolving requirements, and balanced requests from leadership with technical considerations.
- **Testing & Refinement:** Reviewed website functionality, responsiveness, and usability across different screen sizes, addressing issues and polishing the final experience.

## Project Outcome

Delivered a website shaped by continuous collaboration with leadership, combining thoughtful design with practical functionality. The project strengthened my experience in client-facing software development, translating business requirements into technical solutions, and managing the complete website development lifecycle.
